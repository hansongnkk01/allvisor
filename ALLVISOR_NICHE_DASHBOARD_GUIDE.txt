================================================================================
ALLVISOR — PANDUAN DASHBOARD SETIAP NICHE (ADMIN / MANAGER / STAFF)
Berdasarkan apa yang SUDAH dibina dalam project folder allvisor
================================================================================

Cara baca:
- "Staff" = audience staff (role: supervisor / manager / staff) → /staff-dashboard
- "Manager" = Manager Zone dalam sidebar staff (alerts, admin, accounting, lhdn)
  → kena unlock password Admin/Manager Zone dulu
- "Admin" = audience owner/co-admin (role: owner / admin) → /admin-dashboard
  → ada Owner Zone + Admin Zone

Nota penting:
- Sidebar BUKAN “tabs dalam satu page”. Setiap item sidebar = satu page/route.
- Banyak specialty niche guna pattern sama: NicheModulePage = form Add + table list (CRUD nipis).
- Feature yang ada di capability/DB tapi tiada page penuh akan disebut di bahagian “Belum ada di frontend”.


================================================================================
A. OVERALL DASHBOARD LOOK (SANGAT DETAIL)
================================================================================

1) Layout keseluruhan (AppShell)
- Desktop: grid 2 kolum — kiri sidebar tetap ~260px, kanan content utama.
- Sidebar sticky penuh tinggi skrin, boleh scroll jika nav panjang.
- Atas sidebar: logo klinik/bisnes (kalau ada) + wordmark Allvisor + nama organization.
- Bawah brand: Language Switcher (BM / EN) + butang Log out.
- Main content: padding luas, stack vertical (jarak ~1.25rem antara section).
- Kalau akaun ada lebih dari 1 branch/org: BranchBar muncul atas content untuk switch branch.
- Mobile: sidebar hilang → top bar dengan hamburger + logo org; sidebar jadi drawer overlay.

2) Warna & mood
- Default: background kertas hangat (cream/paper #f6f4f1 → #efeae4), teks gelap (#1c1b19),
  muted gray-brown, garis default teal (#0f766e).
- Card/surface: semi-glass putih translucent, border line lembut, radius mengikut niche.
- Button: primary (accent filled), soft (accent soft), ghost.
- Setiap niche tukar theme via data-niche:
  clinic teal mint | retail orange shop | gym dark rose | salon pink | pharmacy emerald |
  optical sky | tuition blue notebook | workshop orange | vet lime | fashion black editorial |
  electronics dark cyan | wholesale slate | laundry sky | physio sage | lab indigo |
  fnb amber kitchen | hotel gold | property indigo | courier yellow hi-vis |
  contractor amber site | manufacturing dark cyan | legal navy formal | events purple stage |
  farm green earthy.

3) Sidebar navigation
- Dibahagi section label uppercase kecil (Operations / Settings / Manager Zone atau
  Owner Zone / Admin Zone).
- Setiap link ada icon Lucide + label (vocab ikut niche: Patients / Customers / Members / Students).
- Active link highlight; click ada soft transition (opacity pending).
- Bila Manager/Admin Zone unlocked: muncul butang “Exit Admin/Manager Zone”.

4) Staff Dashboard (/staff-dashboard) — floor view
Susunan atas → bawah:
a. Header: “Welcome, {nama}” + subtitle nama org.
b. Strip KPI 2×2 (surface cards) SEBELAH Dashboard AI tips panel:
   - Niche appointment (bukan POS): Appointments today
   - Niche POS: Sales today (MYR)
   - Unpaid invoices count
   - People count (Patients/Customers/Members/Students)
   - Low stock count
c. AI panel staff: tip rule-based (bukan chat LLM) — chip hijau/merah/biru tentang
   low stock, unpaid, cashflow, TIN/LHDN, appointments.
d. Daily Close panel: checklist tutup hari (income, unpaid, no-show ATAU txn count,
   low stock names, LHDN pending/rejected) + quick links.
e. Quick action buttons: Customers, Invoices, (+ Appointments / POS kalau capability ada).
f. NicheCardGrid: kad ringkas ikut capability niche (cash session, classes, jobs, dll).
g. Kalau ada appointments: timetable jam untuk hari ini.
h. Grid: recent invoices + (today sales kalau POS / upcoming appointments kalau care).
i. Kalau POS: top sellers.
j. Ops Brain: StaffMyScoreCard + StaffMyTasksCard (score & task sendiri).

5) Admin Dashboard (/admin-dashboard) — owner oversight
Susunan card (capability-driven):
a. Header welcome + subtitle owner oversight.
b. Headline KPIs: today collected, month profit, overdue receivables, team size.
c. AI Supervisor briefing (full width) — daily briefing.
d. Alerts inbox (half) + Staff ranking (half).
e. Revenue trend chart 14 hari (full).
f. Month P&L (half) + Receivables (half) → link ke Money.
g. Niche-specific oversight grid (contoh: class fill, membership churn, cash variance,
   stock value, table turnover — bergantung capability).
h. Staff activity table → Team.
i. Tasks (half) + Team/Branches (half).
j. LHDN card (kalau ada) + Marketing plays cards.
k. Floating AiChatWidget (owner chat) muncul di sudut bila Ops Brain on.

6) Manager Zone vs Admin Zone vs Owner Zone
STAFF sidebar:
  Operations → (Settings jika commerce) → Manager Zone [Alerts, Admin, Accounting, LHDN]

OWNER/CO-ADMIN sidebar:
  Operations → (Settings) → Owner Zone [Alerts, Team, Performance, Money, Marketing]
  → Admin Zone [Admin, Categories (kalau perlu), LHDN]
  Nota: Owner TAK nampak Accounting di sidebar — diganti oleh Money.

Password gate:
- Admin / Accounting / LHDN / Alerts / Categories guna unlock password yang sama.
- Default password hint biasanya {NamaOrg}{tahun}.
- Unlock simpan cookie zone.


================================================================================
B. SHARED PAGES — INTERFACE YANG DIPAKAI RAMAI NICHE
================================================================================

--- Dashboard (Staff) ---
Fungsi: ringkasan kerja harian floor.
Interface: KPI tiles, AI tips, Daily Close, quick actions, niche cards, lists (lihat A.4).

--- Dashboard (Admin/Owner) ---
Fungsi: oversight bisnes, wang, team, risiko.
Interface: headline KPIs, AI briefing, alerts, ranking, charts, niche oversight (lihat A.5).

--- Customers / Patients / Members / Students ---
Fungsi: CRM — simpan orang yang berurusan dengan bisnes.
Interface:
a. Form Add: name, IC, email, phone, address (required), notes.
b. Clinic/vet/physio: risk level + allergies.
c. Tuition: checkbox subjects untuk enroll.
d. Searchable list: edit/delete, buka timeline (visits/invoices).
e. History zone kuning: deleted customers + activity log section.

--- Appointments ---
Fungsi: book & urus jadual (clinic/salon/optical/vet/physio/lab).
Interface:
a. Toggle Calendar / List + month navigation.
b. Calendar day cells dengan badge bilangan appointment.
c. Pilih hari → DayHourTimetable (slot jam) + booking panel:
   search patient, pilih service category, notes, Book now / reset.
d. List mode: card setiap appointment — status select, complete tick, edit
   (starts/ends/status/notes), delete.
e. Activity log bawah.

--- Invoices ---
Fungsi: bil, bayaran, print, LHDN.
Interface:
a. Day filter + search; senarai invoice berwarna ikut status (paid/unpaid/void/LHDN).
b. Click → modal/portal: line items, record payment, edit status, print,
   LHDN submit/refresh/cancel (bergantung role/plan/TIN), cost extras.
c. Closing unpaid minta early-exit reason.
d. Activity log.

--- Inventory ---
Fungsi: stok produk, adjust, cycle count, smart reorder.
Interface:
a. Smart reorder table + dead stock table.
b. Cycle count start/submit.
c. Frequently used stock.
d. Add product form (barcode HID scanner support; commerce: category, sold_by, image, POS flags).
e. Stock table adjust/select + activity log.

--- POS ---
Fungsi: jualan counter → invoice + potong stok.
Interface:
a. Kiri: New ticket / held tickets, search/barcode, category chips, frequent products, product list Add.
b. Kanan: cart qty steppers, pilih customer, payment method (cash/card/ewallet/transfer),
   Hold / Void / Checkout, success + last invoice link.
c. Empty cart message; out-of-stock error; price-on-sale prompt.

--- Cash ---
Fungsi: buka/tutup laci duit + gerakan cash.
Interface:
a. Open float form.
b. Cash in/out movements semasa session open.
c. Close session: counted cash → variance.
d. Session history table.

--- Receipts ---
Fungsi: cari & semak resit jualan.
Interface:
a. Filter date / employee / customer.
b. List + detail (lines, payments, print, refund).

--- Categories ---
Fungsi: service categories/items + product categories (password zone).
Interface:
a. Service category/item CRUD (catalog untuk invoice/appointment).
b. Product categories + bulk assign (kalau retail capabilities).

--- Logistics ---
Fungsi: supplier & pergerakan stok gudang.
Interface tabs dalam page:
a. Suppliers — form + table.
b. GRN (Goods Receipt) — terima stok.
c. Adjustments — adjust stok.
d. Transfers — pindah stok.

--- Printers ---
Fungsi: setting resit/sticker.
Interface:
a. Receipt settings + sticker settings.
b. Test print / Bluetooth stub.
c. Sticker preview box.

--- Alerts (Manager/Owner) ---
Fungsi: Ops Brain alert queue (refund_rate, voids, cash variance, stock leak, dll).
Interface:
a. Status filter chips.
b. Severity chips.
c. Set investigating / resolved.
d. Empty state bila tiada alert.

--- Admin (Manager/Admin Zone page) ---
Fungsi: setting bisnes sensitif + team tools + import + branch.
Interface (selepas unlock):
a. Lock screen dulu: password form + default hint.
b. Pending branch-link approve/reject.
c. Detection thresholds (Ops Brain sensitivity).
d. Notification channels Telegram/WhatsApp (owner/admin only untuk edit org-level).
e. Security: tukar Admin Zone password (owner/admin).
f. Business settings: nama, phone, address, TIN, SST, dll (owner/admin).
g. Hours / service charge (clinic-style settings).
h. Gym only: Walk-in packages CRUD (name, minutes, price).
i. Logo editor.
j. Invoice number format.
k. Data Import panel (CSV/Excel).
l. Create branch / link existing branch.
Nota: Staff management detail lebih lengkap di Owner → Team; Admin page fokus settings.

--- Accounting (Manager Zone) ---
Fungsi: buku kasar ringkas untuk manager floor.
Interface:
a. Period chips.
b. KPI income / expense / profit.
c. Cash chart.
d. Add income / expense forms.
e. Paged expense + ledger tables + activity.

--- Money (Owner Zone) ---
Fungsi: overview wang owner (ganti Accounting di sidebar owner).
Interface:
a. Bookkeeping sama seperti accounting.
b. Expense-by-category.
c. Receivables current/overdue tables.
d. Open unpaid invoices list.
e. Tiada password zone (owner page terus).

--- LHDN ---
Fungsi: MyInvois e-Invoice Malaysia.
Interface:
a. Mode banner (intermediary/sandbox/production).
b. How-to steps.
c. TIN / intermediary link form.
d. Submissions table (status accepted/rejected/pending).
e. Activity log.

--- Team (Owner) ---
Fungsi: urus staff + ranking prestasi.
Interface:
a. Add/kick staff ikut hierarchy role.
b. Staff score ranking dengan ScoreBars.
c. Recalculate scores.

--- Performance (Owner) ---
Fungsi: prestasi bisnes & staff ikut julat masa.
Interface:
a. Range chips (week/month/…).
b. KPIs vs prior period.
c. Revenue chart.
d. Staff scores.
e. Branch scorecard kalau multi-branch.

--- Marketing (Owner) ---
Fungsi: playbook idea marketing ikut niche.
Interface:
a. Static cards (title + body) dari marketingPlays.
b. TIADA send campaign UI dalam app (lead email ada di funnel /start, bukan page ni).

--- NicheModulePage (pattern specialty) ---
Fungsi: CRUD nipis untuk modul niche.
Interface SELALU sama:
a. PageHeader title + subtitle.
b. Surface “Add”: grid fields + Save.
c. Surface table: columns mentah dari DB, max 100 rows, empty “No records yet.”
d. Tiada search advanced, status board, atau workflow kaya.
