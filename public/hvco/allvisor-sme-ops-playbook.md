## Trap 1 — Using generic software for a niche business

**Symptom:** Your “patients” are labelled customers. Your “classes” look like appointments. Staff get confused; reports lie.

**Fix:** One organisation = one niche. Menus, words, and workflows must match how you actually work.

## Trap 2 — Spreadsheet cashflow theatre

**Symptom:** Unpaid invoices hide in Excel. You only notice when rent is due.

**Fix:** Live unpaid totals on the dashboard + daily close checklist.

## Trap 3 — Ignoring LHDN until the deadline panics you

**Symptom:** TIN missing, messy invoice data, last-minute MyInvois scramble.

**Fix:** Build LHDN-ready invoice habits early. Store TIN / SST correctly per branch.

## Trap 4 — Mixing walk-in retail with care notes in one messy POS

**Symptom:** Salon/clinic sells products but treatment history is nowhere.

**Fix:** Hybrid niches need both booking + POS — without stuffing everything into one “sales” screen.

## Trap 5 — Stock that doesn’t match the shelf

**Symptom:** POS sells items that aren’t tracked; month-end inventori is a fight.

**Fix:** Stock movements tied to sales, adjustments, and low-stock alerts.

## Trap 6 — No ownership of “who owes what”

**Symptom:** Staff argue over partial payments and handwritten IOUs.

**Fix:** Invoice statuses (unpaid / partial / paid) + payment methods on every receipt.

## Trap 7 — Niche bleed across branches

**Symptom:** Clinic language appears inside a gym or retail branch of the same group.

**Fix:** Isolate dashboards and vocabulary per organisation niche.

## Trap 8 — Teaching the team a new language every tool change

**Symptom:** Training takes weeks because buttons don’t match the job.

**Fix:** Vocabulary that matches the niche (patients, members, buyers, students…).

## Trap 9 — No end-of-day ritual

**Symptom:** LHDN pending, unpaid invoices, and empty cash drawer checks wait until “tomorrow”.

**Fix:** Daily close strip: income, unpaid, LHDN pending/rejected, quick links.

## Trap 10 — Buying features you will never open

**Symptom:** You pay for manufacturing modules inside a tuition centre account.

**Fix:** Capability-gated menus — only what your niche needs.

## Trap 11 — No risk reversal when you try software

**Symptom:** You fear locking into a 12-month contract before you know it fits.

**Fix:** Start free, pick your niche, and prove the fit on real invoices before you scale.
